vol. 01 / projects nov 2026

MONOLITHIC TRAVERTINE RESIDENCE SET TO ANCHOR CAPE TOWN COASTLINE

SHAW Interiors' latest private commission explores raw stone proportion and deep coastal thresholds, designed to age quietly alongside the Atlantic landscape.

Positioned along the wind-carved granite slopes of the Atlantic Seaboard, SHAW Interiors' latest private residential commission is conceived as an exercise in geological permanence. Rather than competing with the dramatic ocean horizon, the architecture recedes into a series of cantilevered stone planes and sheltered courtyards.

Every interior threshold has been calibrated to temper harsh western glare. Honed unfilled travertine slabs run continuously from external terraces into the primary living volumes, dissolving the boundary between shelter and landscape while allowing shifting daylight to animate the raw pores of the stone.

Custom blackened bronze screens and monolithic timber joinery anchor the open plan, creating intimate zones of quiet retreat without interrupting spatial sightlines.

vol. 03 / insights sep 2026

TACTILE HONESTY AND HAND-TROWWELLED FINISHES IN MODERN INTERIORS

Collaborating directly with master stone masons and metalworkers allows raw lime plasters and patinated bronze to define the true atmosphere of a room.

In an era dominated by synthetic uniformity, the human eye instinctively seeks subtle imperfection. When a wall is finished with mineral lime plaster applied by hand, light breaks softly across micro-variations in texture rather than reflecting off a flat painted plane.

Where two materials meet—stone against timber, or brass recessed into plaster—we eliminate decorative trim in favour of crisp shadow gaps that celebrate structural honesty.
